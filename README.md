# Verity Life Solutions: homepage hero options

Static samples of a new top section for the veritylifesolutions.com homepage, "Turning Expenses into Wealth for Individuals and Business Owners". These are drafts for review. Nothing on the live site has changed.

**Current design, new words.** These keep today's hero look (background pattern, Inter Tight headline, orange bar, buttons) and change only the text and the benefit strip:

| Option | Page | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| D | [variant-d.html](variant-d.html) | Four benefits as light orange circles (like A) |
| E | [variant-e.html](variant-e.html) | Four benefits as navy tiles (like C) |

The shared styles are in `assets/current-hero.css`, measured from the live hero.

**New layouts:**

| Option | Page | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| A | [variant-a.html](variant-a.html) | The client's mockup as designed: centered on white, family photo on the right |
| B | [variant-b.html](variant-b.html) | Split layout with a "how it works" card and the IBC Authorized Practitioner seal |
| C | [variant-c.html](variant-c.html) | Premium dark navy with the family photo |

[index.html](index.html) links all five options. Side-by-side images are in `preview/`.

Each page shows the live site header and, below the new section, a screenshot of the current homepage continuing unchanged. Fonts are Inter Tight and Inter (already used on the homepage). Colors are the site orange `#FB7F05` and navy `#122030`. The pages carry `noindex` and `robots.txt` blocks crawlers.
